Research on the innovation process from ideas to product

Biography

I am an Associate Professor at Emory University’s Goizueta Business School, where I research and teach how teams develop new products and services.

My research is grounded in real-world experience. Some projects succeeded. Many did not. Through years of research, at least I’ve come to understand why.

PhD (INSEAD), MS (Stanford), BS (UC Berkeley).
